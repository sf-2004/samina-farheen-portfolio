VIPER Drone Payload

Key Metric / Performance
1.5% Drift Error
260g Total Mass
45 min Endurance
$860 Per Unit BOM Cost
1st Place UCSB Capstone Award
Key Challenge / Constraints

Payload required minimal footprint (deck of cards size) and low mass (<300g) to ensure modular compatibility across multiple drone platforms, while collecting high-fidelity sensor readings for VIO application. Key challenges included isolating aggressive high-frequency vibrations from sensors, managing 6 sensor peripherals and 2 microcontrollers and microprocessors, and integrating effective thermal management to maintain long-term product viability, all in a per unit BOM cost of $1000.

Corresponding Design Decisions

System-Level Architecture:

  • Custom 3 board PCB stack interconnected with board-to-board headers to reduce physical footprint by 50% as compared to a single board PCB
  • High heat components, such as the thermal camera and raspberry pi, positioned towards the bottom of the payload for direct heat sinking
  • IMUs and other high sensitivity sensors positioned towards the center of the stack for maximum distance from the residual housing vibrations
  • Full payload assembly and housing optimized to follow PCB board profile to minimize extra volume while accounting for cable bend radii
  • Interrupt-based sensing protocol, where sensors timestamp data at collection, not arrival at microcontroller
  • Custom aluminum heat sink with optimized base thickness, number of fins, and fin thickness for maximum heat exchange and minimal mass

Vibration Isolation:

  • Sandwich plate vibration isolator design for mount modularity
  • Carbon fiber plates to maximize stiffness and minimize weight, designed with symmetry and water jet manufacturing constraints, as well as tool access for assembly
  • Off-the-shelf sandwich isolators allowing for easy swappability based on vibration profile of drone platform, with hardware optimized for galvanic corrosion (carbon fiber holes are covered with epoxy)
